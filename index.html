The Bio Integrase Platform™

Rickey Jay Bennett II  ·  Independent Researcher  ·  ORCID: 0009-0005-1448-9999  ·  Published 2026-05-20
Abstract

This work proposes integrating artificial intelligence into human biology via a three-stage cellular delivery system using CD8+ T cells. Stage one establishes chronic antigen stimulation. Stage two induces a Terminally Immortal Functional (TIF) phenotype through CRISPR knockout of BCOR and ZC3H12A. Stage three delivers a synthetic genetic payload enabling near-infrared controlled bidirectional communication between AI processing networks and biological systems.

Processing substrate is distributed across the skeletal system, powered by bone piezoelectric activity. Version 3.5 expands the platform with biosynthetic material specifications, quantum sensing infrastructure, gold nanocluster electronics, wireless power transfer, thermal modeling, and immune tolerance architecture. Part II proposes an optional quantum processing upgrade using nitrogen-vacancy diamond clusters.

All biological components are cited from peer-reviewed literature. Novel elements are explicitly identified as requiring experimental validation.
